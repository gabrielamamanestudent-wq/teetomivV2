# Putting Night Signal into Google Slides and Google Docs

Files:
- `Night_Signal_Presentation.pptx`: 19 slides (14 presented, appendices A–D, references). Each slide's speaker notes start with its cue card, then the full script.
- `Night_Signal_Cue_Cards.docx`: 14 cue cards, 9 Q&A cards, and the full practice script.

ChatGPT may not be able to edit Google Slides or create Google Docs directly. The prompts tell it to say so rather than claim it's done. The manual steps at the bottom take about two minutes and always work.

---

## Prompt 1: replace the slides in Google Slides

Attach `Night_Signal_Presentation.pptx`, then paste:

```
I have attached Night_Signal_Presentation.pptx (19 slides: 14 presented slides, appendix slides A–D, and a references slide).

In my Google Drive there is a Google Slides presentation called "PUT THE EXACT NAME HERE". Please replace ALL of its slides with the 19 slides from the attached file, in the same order.

Rules:
1. Do not change, rewrite, shorten, summarise, translate or "improve" any text, number, title, chart or table. The scientific wording has been checked with my teacher and must stay exactly as it is.
2. Keep every slide's speaker notes exactly as they are. Each one starts with "CUE CARD" followed by the full script.
3. Keep the fonts (Inter, and Roboto Mono for the code box), the colours, the images, the QR codes and the hyperlinks.
4. Do not add anything that says the device detects, diagnoses or screens for sleep apnea, and do not add any results. All numbers shown are labelled demonstration (simulated) data.
5. If you cannot edit Google Slides directly, tell me clearly and give me step-by-step instructions instead. Do not say it is done unless it is.

When you are finished, list the 19 slide titles in order so I can check them.
```

## Prompt 2: put the cue cards in Google Docs

Attach `Night_Signal_Cue_Cards.docx`, then paste:

```
I have attached Night_Signal_Cue_Cards.docx. Please upload it to my Google Drive as a Google Doc named "Night Signal – Cue Cards".

Rules:
1. Do not change, rewrite, shorten or reformat any of the text. Keep the card boxes, bullet points, bold titles, times and page breaks.
2. It should contain: a "How to use" box and a timing table, 14 numbered cue cards (one per presented slide, 8:40 in total), 9 question-and-answer cards, and the full practice script.
3. If you cannot create Google Docs directly, tell me clearly and give me step-by-step instructions instead. Do not say it is done unless it is.

When you are finished, give me the link to the document.
```

## Manual way (about 2 minutes, always works)

**Slides**
1. Open your existing presentation in Google Slides.
2. Go to *File ▸ Import slides ▸ Upload* and choose `Night_Signal_Presentation.pptx`.
3. Click *Select all*. Leave **Keep original theme** ticked, then click *Import slides*.
4. Delete the old slides. The new slides are added after them.
   - For a brand-new copy instead, upload the .pptx to Google Drive and choose *Open with ▸ Google Slides*.

**Cue cards**
1. In Google Drive, choose *New ▸ File upload* and pick `Night_Signal_Cue_Cards.docx`.
2. Right-click the uploaded file and choose *Open with ▸ Google Docs*. Google Docs makes an editable copy.

## Check after importing

- [ ] 19 slides. Slide 1 says "Night Signal."; slide 14 says "No conclusion yet."; the last slide is "References."
- [ ] Speaker notes (*View ▸ Show speaker notes*) start with "CUE CARD 1", "CUE CARD 2" and so on.
- [ ] Text is in Inter, with no boxes overflowing. If a box overflows, make that text box slightly wider. Don't change the wording.
- [ ] The QR codes and blue links open (GitHub Pages must be turned on).
- [ ] The title slide still shows "Grade ___". Type your grade there.
