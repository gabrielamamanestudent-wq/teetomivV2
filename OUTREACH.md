# TEETOMIC — Course Outreach Kit

Ready-to-send cold-email drafts for signing up Greater Montréal courses to the
Business Corner. Bilingual (EN/FR), written to be **CASL-compliant** (Canada's
Anti-Spam Law). Personalize the `{{placeholders}}` and send.

---

## Before you send — CASL checklist

Canadian anti-spam law applies to these emails. Keep it clean:

1. **Identify yourself.** Every message must include your real name, that you're
   with TEETOMIC, and a way to reach you (this footer is built into the drafts).
2. **Business email exemption.** A first B2B email to a published business
   address (the "info@" or "proshop@" on the course's own website), about
   something relevant to their business, is generally permitted. Use addresses
   the course has published — not scraped personal inboxes.
3. **Unsubscribe.** Give a working opt-out and honour it within 10 business days.
   The footer includes one.
4. **No misleading subject lines.** The drafts say exactly what they are.
5. **Keep records** of who you emailed, when, and any opt-outs.

> Not legal advice — if you scale this up, have a lawyer confirm your process.

Hi {{contact_name}},

I'm {{your_name}}, based in {{your_city}}. I built **TEETOMIC**, a last-minute
"standby list" for Greater Montréal golf. When a course has tee times that would
otherwise go empty, they list them on TEETOMIC and local golfers grab them.

How it works for you:
- **You set the price** on each open slot — we never discount for you.
- **You keep 100% of the green fee.** Golfers pay it at your pro shop, as usual.
- Golfers pay a small **$10 booking fee to TEETOMIC** to hold the spot (that's
  our revenue, not yours) — it doesn't touch your green fee.
- You only list the slots you *want* filled, at least 90 minutes ahead. No
  contract, no software to install — it's a web page.

It's free to join. If you'd like to try it, create your Business Corner here:
{{signup_url}} (access code: **{{business_code}}**). New accounts are approved
by hand, usually same day.

Happy to walk you through it on a quick call — {{your_phone}}.

Cheers,
{{your_name}}
TEETOMIC · {{your_phone}}

_You're receiving this because TEETOMIC helps Montréal-area courses fill empty
tee times. Reply "unsubscribe" and I won't email again._

## Sending tips

- **One at a time, personalized.** Send individually (or mail-merge) — never one
  big CC/BCC blast. It lands better and it's cleaner under CASL.
- **Lead with French or bilingual** for Québec courses; default to French if
  unsure.
- **Target the published pro-shop / info address** on the course's own site.
- **Track replies** in a simple sheet: course, address, date sent, follow-up
  date, outcome.
- When someone signs up, approve them from the email that lands in
  `tomictee@gmail.com`.
